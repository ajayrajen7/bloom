# Style contract v1

Source: Ajay-approved ChatGPT apple sample in `style-anchor-v1.png`.

- Render familiar produce with believable, natural proportions and recognizable real-world structure.
- Use polished digital rendering with smooth modeled shading and subtle natural surface texture. The result should not read as a painting: no visible brush strokes, watercolor, oil-paint texture, canvas grain, or sketch marks.
- Keep highlights soft and restrained enough to avoid a plastic or mirror-gloss look.
- Use clear, clean object contours and enough separation between parts (such as leaf, stem, and body) to remain identifiable when displayed at roughly 180 × 180 pixels.
- Show one complete object centered on a square transparent canvas, with safe and visually balanced margins.
- Keep the background genuinely transparent. Do not add a floor, cast shadow, halo, label, border, or scene.
- Preserve each produce item's own species, silhouette, natural colors, and structure. The apple anchor communicates rendering treatment only; it is not a template for other produce shapes, colors, leaves, or stems.

This contract records visible properties of the approved sample. It does not imply that generated candidates are approved; every candidate still needs Ajay's review.
